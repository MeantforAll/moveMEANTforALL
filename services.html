HOW moveMEANT FOR ALL CAN HELP

Movement that works for you.

Personalized fitness and movement support designed around the individual — not the other way around.

SERVICES

Support that fits where you are.

From one-to-one coaching to in-home training, group programs, and online support, moveMEANT FOR ALL offers flexible ways to get moving.

The starting point is simple: understand your goals, experience, preferences, environment, and what you need to make movement work for you.

Whether you're looking for one-to-one coaching, support in your home, a group experience or online guidance, the approach starts with understanding the person and finding what works.

1:1 Personal Training

Personalized coaching built around your goals, abilities, preferences and starting point.

  • Individualized workouts
  • Strength & movement development
  • Progress at your own pace
  • Adaptations when needed

In-Home Training

Training in a familiar environment can make movement feel more comfortable, accessible and manageable.

  • Training in your home
  • Use of available equipment
  • Environment adapted to you
  • Supportive one-to-one coaching

School & Group Programs

Structured movement experiences for schools, group homes and organizations that want more people participating, moving and having fun.

  • School programs
  • Group home support
  • Activities adapted to the group
  • Participation-focused programming

Community & Small Group Training

Engaging movement experiences for community centres, organizations and small groups, built around participation, connection and enjoyment.

  • Small group sessions
  • Community programs
  • Flexible activities
  • Custom program formats

Online / Remote Coaching

Personalized support for people outside the Tri-Cities who want guidance without needing in-person sessions.

  • Remote coaching
  • Personalized workouts
  • Progress tracking
  • Ongoing guidance and support
