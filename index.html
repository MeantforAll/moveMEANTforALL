THE moveMEANT FOR ALL STORY

Why I believe movement should be for everyone.

moveMEANT FOR ALL grew from years of working with people who needed movement to work differently — and seeing what can happen when they feel understood, supported, and included.

THE moveMEANT FOR ALL METHOD™

Understand the person. Build the movement around them.

Every person starts somewhere different. We begin by understanding what matters to you, then build movement around your goals, experience, environment, and starting point.

The goal isn't to make you fit the program. The goal is to make the program work for you.
